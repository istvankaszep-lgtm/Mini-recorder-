# Mini-recorder-
This is a recorder that can record your voice onto a sd card. Or upload music or any .mp3 format, and play it. I made this bc
i think this is kinda cool, and harder to rout it. It has 3 button's: play, exit, record. 1 pot. meter: for selecting what to
play. It has a oled screen, a usb-c port. the sd card holder. RP2040 chip. Also a AMS1117. I made this proiect with kicad.
